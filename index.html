🛡️ Victim Support & Case Navigation Prototype

One card. One path. Complete support for every victim.

NAVICA gives a victim a single, verifiable identity card for their case — so they always know the stage of their case, what happens next, the rights they hold, and where help is available. Authorities update the case once; the victim sees it instantly.

Zero-Trust Architecture: No sensitive case or medical notes are stored on the physical QR card. Data is securely requested only upon authenticated case login with your personal password.

Architecture & Governance

How NAVICA Works

A conceptual integration layer bridging CCTNS, ICJS, e-Courts, and DLSA to provide real-time victim visibility while enforcing strict zero-trust privacy.

🔐 Core Architectural Safeguards

Data Minimization

Only progress milestones and document notices are retrieved; third-party investigation notes are never exposed.

No Plaintext in QR

Physical cards contain cryptographic tokens only. A lost card cannot leak victim identity or testimony.

Role-Based Access Control

Enforced at server layer: Victims access only their own case; officers see only authorized jurisdictional matters.
